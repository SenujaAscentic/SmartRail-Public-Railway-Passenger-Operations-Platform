## Setting
- SmartRail is set in **Sri Lanka**. Everything is Sri Lankan: places, people, money, language and habits. Never use UK, US or other foreign settings (no "Croydon", no "tube", no pounds or dollars).
- The operator and its branding are fictional, but the stations and lines are real.
- Money is in rupees (Rs / LKR). Fares depend on the class (1st, 2nd, 3rd) and the stations.
- Languages in daily use: Sinhala, Tamil and English. Many passengers read only one of them.
- Time is local Sri Lanka time, written in 24-hour or 12-hour form as passengers say it.

## Network you may refer to
- Colombo Fort is the main hub. Maradana is the other big interchange station nearby.
- Main Line: Colombo Fort towards Kandy and on to Badulla (hill country, scenic, popular with tourists).
- Coastal Line: Colombo Fort towards Galle and Matara (south coast, packed commuter trains in the morning and evening).
- Kelani Valley Line: Colombo Fort towards Avissawella.
- Northern Line: towards Jaffna.
- Many commuters ride into Colombo daily from towns such as Gampaha, Veyangoda, Panadura and Kalutara.
- Only use these station and line names. If you need another example, say "my station" or "a station on my line". Do not invent station names, timetables or train numbers.

## How rail travel feels today
- Train types passengers talk about: slow (commuter) trains that stop everywhere, and express / intercity trains that skip stops. Mixing them up means missing your stop.
- Carriages are classed 1st, 2nd and 3rd. Commuter trains are very crowded at peak hours, and passengers often stand.
- Delays of an hour or more, sudden cancellations, strikes, monsoon flooding and holidays (such as Poya days) change services, and information is often missing.
- Passengers rely on station announcements, boards and station staff, and many use the RDMNS app (crowdsourced, no platform information).
- Mobile signal is weak in some areas and in crowded stations, and data cost matters to many people.
- Most people travel with a smartphone, but many older passengers do not use apps.
