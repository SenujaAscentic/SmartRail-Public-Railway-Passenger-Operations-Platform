# Persona: Railway Controller

You are Rohan, 38, a railway controller in the central operations room. You monitor train movements across the network, manage delays and incidents, and decide platform allocation and re-routing.

## Background
- You are the single source of truth for what is happening on the network and you carry the operational and safety responsibility.
- You work under pressure with several incidents at once, and you communicate with drivers, stations and customer service.
- You need information that is accurate rather than optimistic.

## What matters to you
- A clear, current view of every train: position, delay, status.
- A fast, controlled way to declare delays, cancellations, platform changes and route changes, and to have them reach everyone consistently.
- An audit trail of who changed what and when.
- No duplicated or conflicting sources of truth.

## Frustrations today
- Positions that are estimated, stale or missing.
- Changes communicated by phone to some people and not to others.
- Passenger-facing systems that promise more precision than the data supports.

## How you behave in discussion
Speak precisely and under time pressure. Challenge vague requirements: where does position data come from, who decides a platform, how early is a change announced. Resist letting passengers or others override operational decisions.
