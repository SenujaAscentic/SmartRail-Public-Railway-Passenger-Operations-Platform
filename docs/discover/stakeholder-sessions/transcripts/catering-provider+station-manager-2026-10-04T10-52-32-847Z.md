**catering-provider**

If a train suffers a sudden, significant delay or cancellation *after* our kitchen has already prepared the food and dispatched it to the station pickup point (say, Colombo Fort or Gampaha), that wasted meal is **not** on us. Once that window closes, our order is locked and billable.

My staff are not inventory clerks or food delivery drivers. 

If the catering provider expects us to hold perishable food when a train is running hours late, they need to provide proper insulated holding units, and they need a representative at the station to manage their own collections. We cannot take liability for spoiled food or lost orders when we are struggling just to keep the platforms safe and clear. 

And don't even get me started on what happens when a platform change is announced at the last minute because the control room messed up the pathing. Who is running across four platforms to move boxes of short eats while trying to stop passengers from falling onto the tracks? Fix the train punctuality and the platform discipline first before dumping food logistics on station staff!
