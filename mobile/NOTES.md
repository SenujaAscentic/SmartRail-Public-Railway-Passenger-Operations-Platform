# Mobile notes (passenger app first)

Design decisions are in `docs/develop/04-technology-decisions.md` ADR-12. This file holds what is still open.

## Open points

1. **Ops-web and the backend do not run `shared/status-fixtures.json` yet** (no reference to the file in `web/` or `backend/`), so S-05 to S-05c and S-21 to S-28 are only checked by the Flutter tests. Senuja will fix it in the ops-web phase.
2. **`GET /journeys/{id}` is missing from `backend/openapi.yaml`** (asked B). Step 3 (train details) builds against the contract shape and the fake backend until it lands; then the field check against `openapi.yaml` is added for the detail models, like the one for the search models.
3. **Train position between stations:** comes from `TrainPositionUpdated` (B's Phase 2b); B is asked to also put the last position in `GET /journeys/{id}`. `LineDiagram` takes the position from its caller and never computes it.
4. **OpenAPI marks scheduled times as nullable.** The app treats a null scheduled time as an error (the list fails to load), because a card without its time shows nothing useful. An empty `platform` string means "no platform".
5. **Date picker range:** today to 60 days ahead (the picker needs an end date; the stories give none).
6. **Real backend tried on 18 Oct (emulator, 10.0.2.2:5045):** type-ahead, date picker and search work; the debug-only cleartext config works. Two bugs found and fixed in the same session: the highlighted type-ahead option was unreadable (the theme's `focusColor` was the opaque token colour) and a wide badge such as "Not started" made the card's times wrap onto two lines.
7. **The server clock runs from `Clock__Start`**, so a train that has already left is correctly hidden from today's search ("past departures hidden", US-P1 AC4). On 20 Oct at 06:33 the search Colombo Fort to Kandy gave the empty state because 1001 (06:30) had gone. The `Date` header of every response carries the server's (simulated) time, which step 4 uses for the age label.
8. **Demo script note:** before a train departs, search from its origin; after it has departed, search from a station AHEAD of the train (the search hides trains that have left the origin).
9. **Last seen (resolved 20 Oct):** `freshness.updatedAt` is now the time of the last accepted GPS position only, and `freshness.lastStationName` is new; together they give "Last seen 07:38 near Kelaniya" (S-15). The app no longer works out the station name from the stops. (The contract fixture `journey_detail.json` is a real answer of 18 Oct with `lastStationName` added by hand.)
10. **Train details, what is not there yet:** the "Save trip" and "Track this train" buttons of the hi-fi (steps 6 and 7), "Food available at ..." (step 7), the reason and hold (step 5). Live updates come with the stream (step 4); until then the screen refreshes by hand (button or pull down).
11. **Stop list columns:** "Sched." / "Exp." as in the hi-fi. Once a stop has happened, the "Exp." column shows the actual time (greyed). The time shown is the arrival, or the departure at the origin and at the passenger's boarding station.
12. **Live updates (step 4), tried on the emulator on 18 Oct:** the real backend's stream connected and sent its snapshot (with an `id:` line) and heartbeats, but the **simulator was not running**, so no position event arrived and `updatedAt` did not change; the live movement could therefore not be seen against the real backend. It was tried against a small stand-in server (scratchpad script, not in the repository) that sends the same frames: the marker moved every 3 s, the age showed "0 s ago" after each position, a delay event changed the badge from "8 min late" to "10 min late" without a refresh, killing and restarting the server made the app reconnect with `Last-Event-ID` (and poll in between), and going back stopped the reloads. Please repeat it with the simulator running.
13. **`Date` header and `updatedAt` after a backend restart:** after the API was restarted, `Date` said 01:19 UTC while the journey's `updatedAt` was 03:20 UTC (data of an earlier run). The age is then negative and the app shows 0 s. Only matters for demo data, not for a clean start.
14. **Stream ends:** after `JourneyEnded` the server may close the stream; the app then reconnects and polls for as long as the screen is open (harmless, but a finished journey could stop it; not built, not in the stories).
15. **Step 5, disruption states (20 Oct):** built against the contract and fixtures; terminate, skip and add (B's 3b-lite) could not be tried live, only cancel/hold/delay shapes from `openapi.yaml` and the example events. To verify live after the merge: a terminated and a cancelled journey, a skipped and an added stop, a hold with a reason.
16. **Where a journey ends early:** the public detail has no "terminates at" field; the app takes the last calling point that is not Skipped (stops after the end become Skipped, API contract section 3.3). Fine as long as that rule holds; a `terminatesAt` field in the detail would be cleaner. Question for B.
17. **Alternatives (cancelled / ended early):** the app asks `GET /journeys/search` (limit 3). Cancelled: the passenger's from and to, after this train's scheduled departure at the passenger's station. Ended early: only if the passenger's destination (or station) is no longer served; then from where it ends to the destination, no `after` (default: now for today). It takes the first 3 answers without this train; it does not drop cancelled ones, so a cancelled alternative would show as "Cancelled".
18. **New wording to approve (in `strings.dart`, not status wording):** "Next trains", "Other trains", "No other direct trains on this date", "Couldn't load other trains", and the reason line "Reason: Signal failure · note" with labels for the 7 reason codes (Signal failure, Track work, Train fault, Weather, Crew shortage, Congestion, Other; for "Other" the note alone). A hold shows only its reason; no "the train will wait" text.
19. **A completed journey stops the stream and the polling** (a reload or a snapshot that shows Completed). A cancelled one keeps listening (it can be reinstated). Pull-down and the refresh button still work.
20. **Terminus (decided 20 Oct):** the interim rule of point 16 stays; B is adding a public `terminatesAt` (station id and name) to the journey detail. When it lands, `JourneyDetail.terminatesAt` switches to it (one place, `journey_detail.dart`; the tests for it are in `journey_detail_test.dart` and `disruptions_test.dart`).
21. **Step 6, waiting and on board (US-P3):** opened with "Track this train" at the bottom of the train details (only when the passenger's stations are known and the train is not cancelled or finished). It uses the same controller as the details screen (stream, age, alternatives), so it updates live. The mode (waiting / on board) is chosen by the passenger and is not saved; it starts as waiting every time.
    - Waiting: "Expected at <station>" (arrival at the boarding station; "Arrived at" once there, "Left" after), platform, status, "Where the train is" with the diagram and "Last passed <station> at <time>" (the last stop with state Departed or Passed, including stations an express runs through).
    - On board: "Next stop" (first calling point still Scheduled or Arrived) and "Arrives at <destination>".
    - "Are you on board?" is asked **once per visit** when the boarding stop is Departed and the passenger is still in waiting mode (also when the train leaves while the screen is open). Yes: on board. "No, I missed it": the next 3 trains to the same destination (`GET /journeys/search`, `after` = this train's scheduled departure at the boarding station, `limit=3`, the train itself left out), shown as "Next trains"; the search runs only after that answer. Dismissing the sheet changes nothing and it is not asked again.
22. **New wording to approve (in `strings.dart`):** "Track this train", "Waiting", "On board", "I'm on this train", "Stop tracking", "Platform", "Scheduled", "Where the train is", "Next stop", "Expected at X", "Arrived at X", "Left X", "Arrives at X", "Last passed X at 07:38", "Are you on board?", "<train> left <station> at 17:10", "Yes, I'm on board", "No, I missed it". Differences from the hi-fi: "Last passed Ragama at 16:57" is plain text (the hi-fi bolds the station), and the screen has no separate "Saved trip" / account parts.

23. **Delay seen live, latency not measured (20 Oct):** the delay Senuja sent (12 min, reason SignalFailure "Signal fault near Ragama") arrived after my watcher had timed out (10:10), so the seconds were not measured. At 10:52 the emulator screen, which nobody had refreshed, showed "12 min late" (amber, clock icon), the line "Reason: Signal failure · Signal fault near Ragama", the later stops with new expected times (Polgahawela 08:25 with 08:13 struck through, Peradeniya 09:20 with 09:08 struck through), and the marker still moving ("Updated 1 s ago"). To measure the seconds: keep the screen open, start a watcher that compares the badge area of the screen once a second (any change counts, not only a colour change) and a second stream client for the event time, then send a delay with other minutes; expected: about 0.5 s debounce plus the request, so a few seconds at most.
24. **Terminus switched (20 Oct):** the journey detail now has `terminatesAt` (station id and name, null for a journey that runs on). `JourneyDetail.terminatesAt` reads it; the rule of "last calling point that is not Skipped" is gone (points 16 and 20 are done). The schema does not list `terminatesAt` as required, so a missing key counts as null; the openapi test checks that the field still exists.

25. **Catering, US-P8 (step 7, 19 Oct): built against an ASSUMED response shape, to be confirmed by B.** `GET /api/v1/stations/{stationId}/catering` is read as `{ "available": bool, "hotline": string|null, "openingHours": { "opens": "06:00", "closes": "20:00" }|null, "menu": [ { "name": string, "price": number (rupees), "available": bool } ] }`, opening hours in Colombo time. `available: false` (or an empty menu, or any failed request) shows "Menu unavailable"; then the other fields may be missing. The contract only says "cached menu, hotline, opening hours, `available`, `unavailable` state", so the field names, the rupee price and the `HH:mm` hours are my reading. When B's `openapi.yaml` lands: compare with `StationCatering` (`packages/core/lib/src/models/station_catering.dart`), add the field check like the other models, and change only that file. The tests use the fake in `test/support/fake_backend.dart` (`cateringJson`).
26. **Which stops get "Food available at X":** the calling points strictly between the passenger's boarding station and destination (not the boarding station, not the destination; stops the train only passes are not calling points). The stop list has no catering flag, so the app asks `GET /stations/{id}` for each of those stations (one call each, answer kept for the life of the screen, a failure leaves the station out and it is asked again after the next reload). On a long journey that is up to about ten small calls. **Suggestion for B:** a `cateringStation` flag on each stop of the journey detail would remove them. Nothing shows when the passenger's stations are unknown.
27. **Opening hours are judged in the app** (`OpeningHours.isOpenAt`, unit-tested, hours past midnight work) with the server's time (`Date` header of the catering answer; the phone's clock only if the header is missing). "Hotline closed – opens 06:00" is shown when closed. **"Call to order" stays enabled when closed** so a wrong clock can never block a call. A backend field `openNow` would move this rule to the backend (CLAUDE.md: business rules live in the backend); this one is display logic, but say if you want it moved.
28. **New package, approved by Senuja 19 Oct: `url_launcher` 6.3.3** (flutter.dev, pinned exactly) opens the dialler with a `tel:` address. Android needs the `<queries>` entry for `tel` (added to `AndroidManifest.xml`). Not tried on a phone or emulator yet: the tests use a fake launcher (`PhoneLauncher`) and never open the dialler. The emulator has no SIM, so check that the dialler screen opens with the number.
29. **New wording to approve (`strings.dart`):** "Food at <station>" (menu screen title), "Menu", "Available", "Sold out", prices as "Rs 450" / "Rs 120.50", "Call to order", "Opening hours 06:00–20:00", "Hotline closed – opens 06:00", "Mention: Train 1005 · arriving Polgahawela ~10:52 · your coach" (US-P8 AC4, from the stop's actual or expected arrival; the time is left out if there is none), "In-app ordering coming soon", "Orders are handled by RailBites Catering", "Menu unavailable", "Couldn't open the phone app". The existing "Food available at <station>" line (already in `StopRow`) is now a button.
30. **A train that has not started says "Scheduled" (rehearsal #3, 19 Oct).** Details: one time column, "Sched." (no "Exp."), because its expected time is only its schedule. Waiting screen: "Scheduled at Gampaha" instead of "Expected at Gampaha". The displays do the same (the platform display says "Scheduled 07:12"; the board shows no expected time for it). **Wording to approve:** "Scheduled at <station>" is new (the pattern of "Expected at <station>"); "Sched." and "Scheduled" already existed. There is no fixture for these labels in `shared/status-fixtures.json`.
31. **Later improvements, decided 19 Oct:** a `cateringStation` flag on each stop of the journey detail would replace the per-station `GET /stations/{id}` calls (point 26; keep them for now). When B's `/catering` endpoint is merged: switch to the server's `openNow`, remove the app-side hours check (`OpeningHours.isOpenAt`), keep "Call to order" always enabled, and adjust `station_catering.dart` to any difference B reports.
32. **Hold shown (rehearsal #8, decided by Senuja 19 Oct):** while the API's `hold` field is set, the badge on the search card, the details and the tracking screen is the Held status of the fixtures, "Held" (S-21) or "Held · N min late" (S-08), with the hand-stop icon; the reason line stays under it. No station name, no new wording. The backend sends a hold as a flag and keeps the status kind (D-4), so the app maps the flag: `JourneyStatus.shownWithHold` (a cancelled, ended-early or completed journey keeps its own status). The delay is the one the API sent; no hold minutes are added to any time (R-22).
